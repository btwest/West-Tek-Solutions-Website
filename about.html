Who We Are

WEST-TEK SOLUTIONS exists to explore the capabilities of 3D printing and additive manufacturing in aerospace.

The Mission

Push the limits of additive manufacturing in aerospace — designing and flying our own rockets, creating entertaining and informative videos along the way, and picking up new skills with every build.

Why We Do It

To explore the skies, to challenge ourselves, to learn and grow, and because it's fun.
